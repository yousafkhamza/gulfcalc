How much gratuity will you actually get when you leave your job in the UAE?

Most people find out on their last day, from a figure HR gives them. Few know how it's calculated, or whether they've been paid the right amount.

So I built gulfcalc: free salary and end-of-service calculators for the UAE and the GCC.

🔹 UAE gratuity under the 2022 Labour Law (21 days for the first 5 years, 30 days after, 2-year cap)
🔹 Free zones (JAFZA, DMCC and others), DIFC DEWS, and federal government
🔹 Leave salary, including contracts that count 24 or 22 working days
🔹 Overtime, notice period and a full final-settlement breakdown
🔹 Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain (SIO) and Kuwait
🔹 VAT, loan EMI (flat vs reducing rate) and Dubai mortgage costs

Every result shows its working and the law article it's based on, so you can check it with HR yourself.

It's in English and Arabic, needs no sign-up, and everything is calculated in your browser. Your salary never leaves your device.

On the engineering side: a static site with no backend, rules covered by unit and stress tests, every page checked automatically before deploy, and continuous delivery to GitHub Pages.

Try it here: https://yousafkhamza.github.io/gulfcalc/

If you work in HR or payroll in the Gulf, I'd value your feedback, especially on edge cases.
